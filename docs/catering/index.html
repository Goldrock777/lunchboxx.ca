Frequently Asked — Catering

How many people does the Catering Boxx feed?

15 to 20 people comfortably — 30 Loaded Dogs is roughly 1.5–2 dogs per person, plus fries and pop.

How much lead time do you need?

24 hours is ideal. Same-day is often possible for weekday lunches — order through the main site and add your target delivery time in the notes, and we'll confirm if our kitchen can hit it.

Do you deliver catering outside Surrey Central?

Free delivery is within our 1.2km Surrey Central zone. For catering beyond that (north Surrey, Guildford, Fleetwood), DM us and we'll quote a flat delivery fee.

Can I get a custom catering for 60+ people?

Yes. Multiple Catering Boxxes or a custom bulk order — DM @lunchboxx on Instagram with your headcount and event date.

Do you offer vegetarian options for catering?

Not currently — our menu is beef-focused. We'll announce plant-based options when they launch.
